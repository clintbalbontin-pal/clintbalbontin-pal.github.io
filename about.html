I started in 2015 writing news articles and designing collaterals for the Philippine Department of Tourism. Eleven years later the tools have changed a lot, and the job is the same: figure out what a specific group of people needs to hear, then make something good enough that they stop and read it.

My degree is in sociology and anthropology from Ateneo de Manila University, where I received the Ricardo Abad Social Sciences Award. I still use that training. Most marketing problems come down to understanding what a particular audience already believes and what would change their mind.

After the tourism department I worked in university and institutional communications: events and visual content at Ateneo, leadership program content at the Asian Institute of Management, and communications for the Makati Business Club. That period gave me my print, presentation, and photography skills, and a habit of writing for busy, senior readers.

In 2021 I moved into web design as senior art director at a US real estate web studio and led creative on more than 200 custom websites. Since 2025 my work has centered on email, CRM automation, and funnels: Klaviyo for a DTC appliance brand, GoHighLevel for coaches and service businesses, and training a marketing team to run it all to one standard.

How I work

  • One person from plan to launch. The person who writes your strategy also designs the assets, builds them in your platform, and reads the results.
  • Remote from the Philippines. My mornings overlap with Australian business hours and my afternoons with the UK and Europe.
  • Clear updates. You get regular progress notes and a reply to every message within 24 hours.
  • Honest numbers. If a campaign underperforms, I tell you why and what I would change. My funnel case study includes an account that never reached profit, because the diagnosis is the useful part.
