In short

Luxury agents are judged on taste before anyone picks up the phone. I designed custom homepages and brand identities that looked like the homes they sell, then carried them through WordPress production. The work earned consistent five-star client reviews, four of which name me on Google.

The brief, every time

A buyer or seller looks up an agent before calling. The site has to look like the listings, work on a phone, and make it easy to search properties and get in touch. Each client wanted a site that felt like theirs alone, not a template with a new logo.

What I did

For each project I art directed the homepage design in Figma, set the typography and color for the brand, and handed the approved design into WordPress production. From 2024 I also managed production end to end and handled client relationships through Salesforce.

  • Nelson Luxury for Geoffrey Nelson, San Francisco Peninsula: a dark, architectural homepage built around full-bleed property photography.
  • The Vega Group at Optimar International Realty: a bright coastal layout with team photography and featured listings.
  • City to City Commercial Real Estate: a charcoal and copper homepage for an Arizona commercial brokerage, with a desert hero and line-drawn towers behind the intro.
