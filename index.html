FAQ

Working with me, from anywhere.

Where are you based, and who do you work with?

I'm based in the Philippines and work remotely with clients in Australia, the UK, Europe, the Middle East, and the US. Strategy calls, delivery, and reporting all run online.

How does the time difference work?

The Philippines is on GMT+8. My mornings line up with Australian business hours, and my afternoons overlap with the UK and Europe. Every message gets a reply within 24 hours.

Why hire a digital marketing specialist in the Philippines?

You get senior strategy and hands-on execution from one person, at a rate below what an agency or in-house hire costs in Australia, the UK, or the US. The same specialist who plans your campaign also builds it, ships it, and reads the numbers after.

What services do you handle?

Social media content and management, email and CRM automation in GoHighLevel, Klaviyo, and MailChimp, website and funnel builds, SEO, GA4 tracking, and brand design. Each one has its own page under Services.

Can I see results from past projects?

Yes. The case studies cover a Meta and funnel campaign with full numbers, a Klaviyo program for a DTC brand, 200+ real estate websites, and institutional work for the Makati Business Club and the Department of Tourism.
