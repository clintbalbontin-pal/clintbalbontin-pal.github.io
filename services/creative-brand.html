In short

I design the pieces that keep a brand consistent across every channel: logo and identity, annual reports and print, slide decks, and photography. My print and presentation work comes from years in institutional communications. My identity work comes mostly from luxury real estate.

How I run it

  1. Brief and references. I collect what you like, what you dislike, and where the work will be seen.
  2. Directions. You see a small number of distinct directions and pick one to develop.
  3. Refine. The chosen direction gets tested on real layouts, like a social post or a report cover, before it is final.
  4. Deliver. Final files, usage notes, and templates arrive organized and named so anyone can find them.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you design logos from scratch?

Yes. Logo projects start with a short brief and reference board, then move to a few distinct directions before one is refined.

Will the files be ready for my printer?

Yes. Print files go out with bleed, CMYK color, and embedded fonts, and I adjust them to your printer's specs when needed.

Can you design our slide deck?

Yes. I designed the Makati Business Club's 2020 Annual Membership Meeting presentation, a recap of a full year of forums and speakers.

Do you shoot on location?

I shoot event and aerial photography for projects in the Philippines. Examples are in the portfolio gallery.
